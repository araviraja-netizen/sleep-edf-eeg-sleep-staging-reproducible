# Dataset, labels and attribution

Obtain [Sleep-EDF Expanded v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/) separately. Read its [official license page](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/view-license/1.0.0/), which identifies **Open Data Commons Attribution License v1.0**. The official dataset/embedded license text was retrieved during preparation; a bare `LICENSE.txt` URL returned 404 and is not the attribution authority. Dataset licensing does not determine ownership or licensing of this project's original source, articles or model artifacts.

The dataset page permits access subject to its specified license. Its terms address database use, redistribution/derivative databases and attribution/notices, and distinguish database rights from rights in individual contents. This preparation does not assume that this alone resolves every privacy, content, institutional or model-weight release question. Reviewers should use the authoritative terms; the author explicitly approved inclusion of derivative subject counts and split identifiers, while final inventory/publication approval remains pending. No raw EDFs are bundled or automatically downloaded.

SC: 78 subjects, 153 PSG/hypnogram pairs. ST: 22 subjects, 44 pairs, separate inventory only. Pairing uses the first seven filename characters; the eighth identifies the PSG/scorer marker. Subjects are study-qualified and grouped across nights. Fpz-Cz is used; Pz-Oz availability is recorded but no Pz-Oz model experiment is claimed.

Mapping: Wake 0; N1 1; N2 2; original stages 3/4 → N3 3; REM 4. Movement and unknown have no supervised class. Epochs are exactly `[30*k,30*(k+1))` s from verified PSG origin; complete unique coverage and exact native sample boundaries are required. No overhang samples or shifted annotations are manufactured.

Protocol B retains all valid supervised epochs (414,961). Primary A retains up to 60 original epochs before first/after last valid sleep and all valid internal epochs (195,469); only exterior Wake is removed (219,492). Non-Wake counts stay unchanged. Native Fpz-Cz is 100 Hz; filtering is continuous SOS Butterworth 0.3–35 Hz, fourth-order prototype, `sosfiltfilt`, odd padding 27 samples, float64 computation then float32 µV storage. Full-precision saved normalization is fitted on A training samples only; apply it on loading.

The [split files](../evidence/splits/subject_splits.csv) contain public-dataset subject/recording identifiers, no names or demographics. Their inclusion for exact split verification was explicitly approved by the author. The [freeze file](../evidence/splits/subject_split_freeze.json) retains original SHA-256 values. No subject may be moved based on any reported outcome.
