# Availability of curated and withheld evidence

This public export is smaller than the original research archive. Links in curated manuscript copies that originally pointed to excluded local evidence have been replaced with this explicit availability notice, rather than a broken private path. Those copies retain measured text/captions but are not the complete historical publication package.

Included: all scientific source and original scripts/tests; registered model settings; frozen split identities/hashes; overall and saved transition/stable confusion counts; author-approved pseudonymized subject count clusters; original aggregate metrics; checkpoint/export/source hashes; saved primary validation histories; ten selected non-physiological PNG/PDF figure pairs; curated revised manuscript and citation audit; code-only historical notebooks and a separately executed reviewer notebook.

Excluded saved publication figures: preprocessing EEG/PSD (`03_preprocessing`), individual hypnograms (`11_hypnograms`), embedding projections (`12_embeddings`), gradient/Grad-CAM (`13_explanations`), attention (`14_attention`) and subject-ID plots (`15_subjectwise`). These remain unchanged locally. Their appearance in a historical caption is not evidence that their underlying tensors/signals have been cleared for public redistribution. Reproduce them only with separately obtained/approved inputs and actual scripts; no synthetic replacement is presented as measured research.

The raw dataset, processed arrays, original per-epoch protocol manifests, full prediction/probability/feature exports, checkpoint files, source snapshots of every historical run, full reports with machine-specific archives and complete executed historical notebook outputs are not bundled. Their hashes/needed roles are documented where relevant; hashing them is not equivalent to releasing them. Authors can provide a reviewed private/archive route if journal reviewers require that evidence, subject to rights and privacy decisions.

Historical plotting/manuscript generators under `reports/publication_package` and `reports/manuscript_revision` are exposed as actual source, with their original archive dependencies. They are not advertised as runnable solely from the lightweight bundle. `release_tools/figures.py` provides an independent aggregate redraw route. Probability-based statistics and physiological panels require additional inputs listed in the [reviewer coverage table](REVIEWER_VERIFICATION.md).

`notebooks/historical` contains adapted code-only copies; outputs/execution counts have been cleared for privacy and portability, and these adaptations are **not claimed re-executed**. They require the appropriate independently generated/approved workspace artifacts and may still use legacy signed-archive APIs. The new reviewer notebook is the no-EEG, no-training route that is executed and validated for this release.

The private original Phase 1–6 reports and all experiment bytes are preserved. The export lineage records byte-identical source copies versus edited documentation/path/notebook copies. The scoped MIT code license does not grant rights to withheld PhysioNet material or implies that a public archive has already been created.
