# Scientific and release limitations

- One historical SC cohort and one frozen split; only twelve independent final subjects. No external/ST population performance or clinical validation.
- M1 sees one EEG epoch and has 40,501 parameters/one seed; M5/M7 see five centered epochs and have larger, different capacities/three seeds each. Their aggregate contrasts do not isolate recurrence or attention, nor estimate balanced architecture-level seed variability.
- Contextual models improve aggregate macro-F1 but score lower on the defined transition subset. No smoothing, label-noise or physiological mechanism was isolated; no new subset-significance test was added.
- Validation shortlist/checkpoint selection is development selection. Final intervals condition on these fitted seeds and this split; percentile bootstrap coverage with twelve clusters may be unreliable. Three-seed sample SD is not subject uncertainty or an ensemble score.
- Offline label-informed Wake eligibility, zero-phase filtering and future EEG do not demonstrate streaming deployment or autonomous sleep-boundary detection. Context eligibility itself depends on valid annotated rows.
- Historical stage merging does not create modern AASM multi-scorer consensus. Numerical rail/edge flags are not expert artifact truth. N1 remains weak, and class weighting has only one seed per secondary configuration.
- M8/M6 are validation-only. Bounded M8 removals also change capacity/computation and have short one-seed budgets; no gate-only/matched-capacity/full-convergence factorial confirmation exists.
- Parameter counts and partial MACs do not prove deployment energy efficiency. Recorded wall times include differing stopping trajectories, data/export work and uncontrolled thermal/cache state. Other hardware/software need not reproduce them.
- Preserved primary figures/tables reflect saved historical data; curated manuscripts omit links/assets withheld for data/physiological review. Subject-confusion aliases are pseudonyms, not guaranteed anonymous. The author approved inclusion of the subject counts and split identifiers; this does not establish anonymity or institutional/ethics approval.
- Counts reproduce classification and bootstrap outcomes, not probability calibration/ROC/PR, feature geometry or physiological explanations. Those require separately approved detailed evidence or a new independent reproduction.
- Version pins are observed, not a complete portable lock. Full real pipeline is MPS/POSIX scoped; no validated CUDA/Windows or bit-identical MPS claim. Historical tests needing private archives/raw data are explicitly classified as requiring additional inputs.
- Genuine author/copyright metadata and scoped MIT are confirmed; exact final inventory/publication approval remains pending. Local preservation is not a public archive DOI or a journal submission. The proposed release has no public remote and no files have been staged or uploaded.

The original final test cohort is consumed. Further development requires independent evaluation data; recounting or republishing unchanged outcomes does not reset test independence.
